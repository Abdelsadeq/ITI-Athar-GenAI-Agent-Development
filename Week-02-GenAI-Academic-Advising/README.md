# Week 02 — Generative AI in Your Department

## Assignment Overview

This assignment explores a practical application of Generative AI in higher education.

**Project Title:** AI-Powered Personalized Academic Advising and Graduation Planning

The proposed solution aims to help university students understand academic regulations, track their academic progress, and plan future semesters more effectively.

## Problem Statement

Students following the Credit-Hour System often face difficulties understanding course prerequisites, GPA requirements, registration rules, academic warnings, and graduation requirements.

These challenges can make academic planning complicated and increase students' dependence on academic advisors.

## Proposed Solution

An AI-powered academic advising assistant that combines Generative AI with university academic regulations and student academic records to provide personalized guidance.

The proposed system would help students understand their academic standing, explore course registration options, and develop graduation plans.

## Role of Generative AI

Generative AI enables natural-language interaction, personalized explanations, and accessible academic guidance.

University regulations and student records would serve as the foundation for recommendations, while academic advisors would remain responsible for reviewing important decisions.

## Assignment Deliverable

A two-page report describing the proposed Generative AI application, its benefits, limitations, and responsible implementation.

**Format:** PDF
